## How to not get your climbing rope tied up

-- A mathematical perspective

A knot in the mathematical sense is defined using a loop of rope. If -- independent from bending and folding, any manipulation that doesn't affect the integrity of the rope, the loop doesn't turn to a string, it is a knot. In the same way, through the same methods, a string can't truly knot.
